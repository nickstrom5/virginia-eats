Oyster bars

10 oyster bars and raw bars, from the Chesapeake Bay shore to the Blue Ridge. Each is on its official Virginia tourism listing, checked in fall 2026.

From the bay

On the Northern Neck, Fish Hawk Oyster Bar at the Tides Inn roasts oysters on the beach; on the Eastern Shore, the Oyster Farm Seafood Eatery sits on the bay at Cape Charles. In Hampton Roads the raw bars run from Norfolk's Colonial Avenue to Lynnhaven in Virginia Beach, and inland Hank's in Old Town Alexandria, King Street Oyster Bar in Leesburg and Public Fish & Oyster in Charlottesville shuck them too.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Norfolk (2), Virginia Beach (1), Portsmouth (1), Irvington (1), Alexandria (1), Mechanicsville (1), Leesburg (1), Cape Charles (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story, or the Virginia Tourism Corporation's own listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
